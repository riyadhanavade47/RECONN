How Donation Tracking Works

Your contribution can be followed through its journey.

💚

1. Donation Submitted

Your donation details are recorded and a unique Donation ID is created.

🏢

2. NGO Receives It

The verified NGO can acknowledge that your donation has been received.

📸

3. Proof & Update

The NGO can add photo proof and explain how the contribution was used.

📊

4. Track Your Impact

Donors can view the donation status, photos and impact updates in My Impact.

Ways You Can Donate

💰

Donate Money

Record a financial contribution according to an NGO's requirement. The donation can then be tracked through its Donation ID.

📦

Donate Items

Record useful items such as clothing, books, educational materials and other reusable resources. The NGO can later acknowledge receipt and provide an update.

🌱 Donation Transparency

ReConnect records each donation with a unique tracking ID. Once an NGO receives the donation, it can update the status and provide photo proof or an impact update for the donor to view.
