🌱 Reuse First, Recycle Responsibly

ReConnect follows a reuse-first approach. If an item is still useful, it can be directed towards a verified NGO or an appropriate recipient. If the item is damaged, it may be suitable for repair, upcycling or another reuse process. Items that cannot be reused can be directed towards responsible recycling through an appropriate organization or recycling partner.
