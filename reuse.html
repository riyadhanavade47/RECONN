Reuse & Recycle

Instead of throwing useful items away, connect them with organizations that can reuse, upcycle or recycle them responsibly.

What Can You Reuse or Recycle?

Many everyday items can find a second life when they reach the right organization.

👕

Clothes

Donate usable clothes that can help people and communities in need.

📚

Books

Give books a second life through schools, libraries and community programs.

🪑

Furniture

Usable furniture can be redirected to organizations that need it.

🧸

Toys

Safe and usable toys can be reused by children and community centers.

💻

Electronics

Working electronics may be reused, while damaged devices can be responsibly recycled.

📦

Other Items

Other reusable items can be submitted for appropriate routing and evaluation.

How Reuse & Recycling Works

ReConnect helps route items toward their most suitable destination.

1

Submit Your Item

Tell us what you want to donate, including its condition.

2

Condition Check

The item can be evaluated to understand whether it is reusable or recyclable.

3

Smart Routing

Suitable items can be routed toward NGOs or appropriate recycling partners.

4

Track the Impact

Future ReConnect tracking can show what happened to your contribution.

Responsible Reuse

ReConnect aims to reduce unnecessary waste while making sure usable items reach people or organizations that can benefit from them.

🌱

Reuse First

Items that are still useful should be considered for reuse before recycling.

🔄

Upcycle

Some materials may be transformed into useful products instead of becoming waste.

♻️

Recycle Responsibly

Items that cannot be reused can be directed toward appropriate recycling channels.

Have Something Useful to Give?

Every reusable item can become someone else's useful resource instead of ending up as waste.
