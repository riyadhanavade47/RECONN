Where Will Your Item Go?

ReConnect follows a reuse-first approach.

🟢

Good Condition

Items that are clean and usable can be directed towards verified NGOs for donation and further distribution.

Route: NGO Donation
🟡

Damaged but Reusable

Items that can still be repaired or transformed can follow a reuse or upcycling route.

Route: Reuse / Upcycling
🔴

Not Usable

Items that cannot reasonably be reused should follow responsible recycling or disposal guidance.

Route: Recycling Guidance

🌱 Reuse First, Recycle Responsibly

ReConnect encourages users to consider donation, repair and reuse before recycling. Actual partner-based routing can be added when verified reuse and recycling organizations join the platform.
