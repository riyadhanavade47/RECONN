Our Reuse Philosophy

ReConnect follows a simple principle: reuse whenever possible, recycle responsibly when direct reuse is not suitable.

♻️ Reuse First

Items that are clean, usable and suitable can be directed towards organizations that may be able to use them.

🔧 Give It a Second Life

Items that cannot be directly donated may be repaired, transformed or upcycled into useful products.

🌍 Responsible Recycling

Items that cannot reasonably be reused can be directed towards appropriate recycling processes.
