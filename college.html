🎓 College & NSS Community

Connect students and NSS groups with verified community, environmental and social activities published by government departments, NGOs and authorized organizations.

🏛️

Who Creates the Activities?

Official activities can be published by government departments and other authorized organizations. Colleges, NSS groups and students can discover these activities and participate in them.

How College & NSS Participation Works

A simple journey from discovering an activity to recording your contribution.

1

Discover

Find verified activities published by government, NGOs and authorized organizations.

2

Register

Students or NSS groups can register for an activity.

3

Participate

Attend the activity and contribute your time and effort.

4

Track Impact

Verified participation can appear in your My Impact and contribution history.

What College & NSS Groups Can Do

Students and college groups can participate in different types of social and environmental initiatives.

🤝

NGO Collaboration

Connect with verified NGOs and participate in their volunteering and community initiatives.

🌱

Environmental Drives

Participate in tree plantation, beach cleaning, plastic collection and environmental awareness drives.

📚

Teaching & Education

Participate in teaching children, educational support and awareness activities.

🎁

Donation Drives

Participate in organized donation drives and help collect useful items for people and communities.

🩸

Blood Donation

Discover and participate in authorized blood donation camps and community health initiatives.

🐾

Animal Welfare

Participate in animal welfare, awareness and community support activities.
